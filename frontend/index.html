How to Use EchoWave

    
    
      
  • Register/Login: Create an account or log in with your username and password.
    • 
      
  • Join a Channel: Enter a Channel ID (e.g., "room1") and click "Connect" to join the voice
        room.
    • 
      
  • Become Speaker: Click "Request Speaker" to take the mic and broadcast your audio to others in
        the channel.
    • 
      
  • Release Mic: When done speaking, click "Release Speaker" to let others take over.
    • 
      
  • Logout: Click "Logout" to end your session.
    • 
      
  • Tips: Ensure your microphone is enabled. Audio will play automatically from the speaker.
